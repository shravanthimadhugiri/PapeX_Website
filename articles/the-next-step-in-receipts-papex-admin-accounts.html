The Next Step in Receipts: PapeX Admin Accounts

Receipts aren’t just for buyers—they’re for businesses, families, and anyone who needs visibility into spending. With PapeX Admin Accounts, we’re building a future where-receipts move as seamlessly as money itself.

PapeX Team Nov 30, 2025

When we first started PapeX, the mission was simple: receipts don’t belong on paper, in cluttered inboxes, or hidden inside folders that never get opened again. They belong where the rest of our financial lives already live—on our phones, neatly organized, instantly accessible, and shareable when needed.

The first big leap was digitization. Why settle for a crumpled slip of thermal paper that fades after a week, when you could have a permanent, digital copy in your pocket? Then came instant transfer. As we build integrations with POS systems, we eliminate the wait, the scanning, and the middle steps. A purchase is made, and—just like that—the receipt appears. Seamless.

But we always knew that receipts aren’t just about the buyer. They’re about the

who make sense of those-receipts. Bookkeepers trying to close the books at the end of the month. CFOs managing company cards. Parents trying to teach their kids about spending. Families sharing expenses.

That’s why we’re thrilled to be developing our next major feature—one designed to take-receipts beyond the individual and into shared financial management.

What It Means

With admin accounts, receipts don’t just stay with the person swiping the card—they can automatically route to an administrator’s account.

  • For businesses: Imagine your bookkeeper never having to chase down missing receipts again. Every purchase made on a company card is instantly visible in one place. End-of-month reconciliation becomes a matter of minutes, not hours or days.
  • For families: Parents can see transactions in real-time, not weeks later. Whether it’s monitoring a college student’s spending on a family card or helping track shared household expenses, everything flows into the admin’s PapeX account without any extra steps.
  • For the financially savvy: Even outside of work or family, the feature creates a way to centralize purchases across multiple cards. For freelancers juggling personal and client cards, or individuals tracking side-hustle expenses, the visibility is immediate.

Why It Matters

Digitizing receipts was the foundation. Instant transfers were the bridge. But admin accounts are the “unlock”—turning receipts into a collaborative, transparent, and truly useful part of financial management.

This is the future we’ve been building toward since the beginning:

  • Just receipts that move as seamlessly as money itself.

At PapeX, we’re not just digitizing slips of paper—we’re digitizing the

. And this feature makes it clear: receipts aren’t just for buyers. They’re for businesses, families, and anyone who needs better visibility into spending.

What’s Next

As we roll out this feature, we’re excited to see the new ways people will use it. Because every time-receipts become easier to access, share, and manage, financial life gets just a little simpler. And that’s what PapeX has been about from day one.
